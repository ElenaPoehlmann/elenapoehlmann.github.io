I am on the 2026–27 academic job market, seeking opportunities in organization, strategy, or management research. My interests centre on how organizations and institutions shape equal opportunity.

About

I am a PhD Fellow in the Department of Strategy and Innovation at Copenhagen Business School. My dissertation examines how access to leadership positions is structured before and during formal selection, with a focus on gender inequality. Across my research, I am using qualitative and quantitative methods, drawing on original field data.

I hold an MSc in International Business from Maastricht School of Business and Economics and have been a visiting PhD Fellow at the INSEAD Gender Initiative.
